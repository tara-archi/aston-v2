Discover Gili Trawangan: a favourite island escape for international travellers

29 August 20266 min readBy Aston Editorial
HERO IMAGE 3:2 — GILI TRAWANGAN BEACH

Sunset over the north-west coast of Gili Trawangan.

There are no cars on Gili Trawangan. You arrive by boat, step onto white sand, and get around by bicycle or on foot. It is the largest of the three Gili islands off Lombok, and the easiest place to slow down for a few days.

Why Gili T

The island is small enough to cycle around in an hour, but there is plenty to fill the days: snorkelling with turtles a few metres from shore, sunset swings on the west coast, and a relaxed night market for dinner.

Getting There

Fast boats leave from Bali (Padang Bai or Serangan) and take about two hours. From Lombok, public boats run from Bangsal harbour in around 20 minutes. Book a return ticket and check the sea conditions in the rainy season.

Three Slow Days

Start with a sunrise swim, hire a bicycle for the loop around the island, and save the west coast for sunset. On day two, snorkel at Turtle Point and book a late lunch on the beach. Keep the last day free for a long breakfast and one more swim.
